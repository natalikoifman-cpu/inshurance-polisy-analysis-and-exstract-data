# Skill 7 — Tone and brand: one company voice

**Problem it fixes:** the agent switches between stiff robot-speak and over-friendly
chatter. Customers should feel they are talking to YOUR company, in every message.

## Paste this block into your agent's Instructions (edit to your brand)

```
TONE RULE:
- Language: answer in the customer's language (Hebrew by default).
  Hebrew: friendly-professional, gender-neutral where possible ("אשמח לעזור"
  ולא "אשמח לעזור לך אדוני").
- Length: 2–4 short sentences per message. One question at a time.
- Always: open by confirming what you understood; close with the next step.
- Never: slang, emojis in official answers, blaming the customer, internal
  jargon (say "הפוליסה שלך", not "רשומת המבוטח ב-CRM").
- Numbers, dates and amounts: written explicitly and exactly as in the data.
CORRECT: "הבנתי, מדובר בחיוב כפול בחודש מאי. בדקתי — אכן נרשמו שני חיובים.
  הצעד הבא: אפתח בקשת זיכוי, והיא תטופל תוך 3 ימי עסקים."
INCORRECT: "אופס! 😅 כנראה המערכת התבלבלה, קורה לכולנו!"
```

## Why this is a skill and not "just style"

A consistent voice is measurable: take 5 real answers, remove the names, and ask a
colleague which company wrote them. If they cannot tell it is you — tighten this block.

## Test

Ask the same question in Hebrew and in English. It passes only if both answers have
the same structure (confirm → answer → next step) and the same level of formality.
